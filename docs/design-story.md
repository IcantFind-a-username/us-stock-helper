# US Stock Helper — Calm Alpha Design Story

Generated: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

US Stock Helper reduces a noisy global information stream into an auditable
mobile decision path for short-term, swing, and long-term US equity research.
The first view defaults to short-term analysis.

## Audience

The primary user actively trades US equities, values speed and high potential
returns, and wants early warning without surrendering objectivity or control.

## Narrative

The product tells a simple story:

1. **What is the market doing?**
2. **What deserves attention now?**
3. **What evidence supports or contradicts the idea?**
4. **What would a bounded plan look like?**
5. **What could make the plan wrong?**

The deep navy market card represents the calm control room. White evidence
surfaces represent inspection and verification. Green and red are used
sparingly so market meaning remains immediate.

## Emotional journey

- Entry: calm orientation rather than alarm.
- Exploration: efficient scanning with clear priority.
- Engagement: confidence from evidence, freshness, and explicit uncertainty.
- Peak: a decision plan that remains reversible and auditable.
- Exit: the user retains control and manually decides whether to trade.

## Core visual metaphor

**A quiet institutional research desk in a pocket.**

It avoids casino-like neon, constant motion, and unsupported certainty. The
assistant can be fast and direct while still showing what it knows, what it
does not know, and why it may be wrong.

## Three key experiences

### Dashboard

The user first sees a market verdict, recommended posture, and risk ceiling.
The strongest alert follows, then the moomoo watchlist and discovery candidates.

### Stock detail

The user moves from price and candlesticks into indicators, forecast ranges,
ownership/activity proxies, macro context, and a deterministic long or short
plan. Technical indicators explain context but never become automatic orders.

### Adviser council

Thirteen investor-style simulations debate the same evidence snapshot. The
experience makes disagreement visible, allows abstention, and lets advisers
request more research. Style gives the explanation texture; evidence remains
the authority.

## Micro-interactions

Selections settle with short ease-out transitions. Evidence drawers slide and
fade in without displacing the user’s reading position. Charts respond quickly
but never pulse continuously. Reduced-motion mode preserves all state changes
without animation.

## Accessibility story

The interface treats readable type, large touch targets, chart summaries,
semantic labels, and non-color state cues as foundational—not optional polish.

## Intended impact

The user should feel faster but not rushed, informed but not falsely certain,
and supported without being controlled. Success is measured by evidence
coverage, comprehension, disciplined execution, calibrated alerts, and fewer
avoidable mistakes—not by how often the interface says “buy.”
